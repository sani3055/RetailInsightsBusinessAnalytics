# Retail Data Warehouse: Executive Business Summary

**Date:** September 2026
**Data Source:** Olist E-Commerce Dataset (100k+ Orders)
**Objective:** Identify revenue drivers, operational bottlenecks, and customer retention trends to inform Q4 strategy.

---

## 1. Revenue Growth is Decelerating
**Observation:** After a period of explosive Month-over-Month (MoM) growth throughout 2017, revenue growth plateaued and began to contract in early 2018. 
**Impact:** While total revenue volume remains high, the lack of accelerating growth indicates market saturation or a drop in marketing efficiency.
**Recommendation:** Pivot strategy from aggressive new-customer acquisition to maximizing the Lifetime Value (LTV) of the existing customer base through loyalty programs or subscription models.

## 2. Regional Dominance (The "SP" Reliance)
**Observation:** São Paulo (SP) drastically outpaces every other state in gross revenue, driven primarily by the *Bed, Bath & Table* and *Health & Beauty* categories. Other major states like Rio de Janeiro (RJ) and Minas Gerais (MG) are trailing significantly.
**Impact:** The business is heavily over-indexed on a single state. Any localized economic downturn or supply chain disruption in SP would severely impact top-line revenue.
**Recommendation:** Launch targeted, geo-fenced marketing campaigns in RJ and MG offering subsidized freight for high-margin categories (like Health & Beauty) to balance regional revenue dependency.

## 3. The Delivery Bottleneck
**Observation:** Operational analysis reveals a significant percentage of orders arrive past their estimated delivery date, particularly in northern and western states where logistics networks are less dense.
**Impact:** Delivery delays are directly correlated with 1-star reviews, which depresses seller ratings and drives customer churn.
**Recommendation:** Renegotiate Service Level Agreements (SLAs) with carrier partners in high-delay states, or implement a dynamic "estimated delivery" algorithm that adds a 2-3 day buffer for rural zip codes to manage customer expectations.

## 4. Severe Churn Post-First Purchase
**Observation:** Cohort analysis shows a steep drop-off in active customers immediately following their first purchase month. Long-term retention (Month 3 and beyond) is in the low single digits.
**Impact:** The business operates as a transactional marketplace rather than a sticky ecosystem. Acquisition costs are not being amortized over repeat purchases.
**Recommendation:** Implement an automated CRM lifecycle campaign. Trigger a targeted discount email 21 days after a customer's first purchase (specifically cross-selling accessories related to their initial purchase) to force the second purchase and establish a buying habit.
